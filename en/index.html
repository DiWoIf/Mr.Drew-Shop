FAQ

Can I create my own design?

Yes, that is our specialty. Send us your design, and we will choose the best printing method (DTF or screen printing) to bring your vision to life.

What if I only have an idea but no ready-to-print artwork?

No problem at all. Just describe it to us in words, and we will help you visualize it and prepare the files for printing.

How long does it take?

Typically 1–3 business days for production. On top of that, courier delivery time varies by city, but we work at express speed.

Do you ship to Europe?

Yes. We ship across all of Europe with no extra hassle.

Can I order just a single item?

Of course. One, ten, or a hundred — whatever works best for you.

Will the print wash off?

No. We make our products to be worn for years, not just once. We use durable printing techniques (DTF) that can withstand countless washes.

Do you print on products other than t-shirts?

Yes! We print on hoodies, cotton tote bags, hats, socks, and mugs. For every material, we select the most durable printing technique available.

How to order?

Contact us directly or place an order through our website. We will guide you through every step of the way.
